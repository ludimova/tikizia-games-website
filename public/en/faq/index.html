Account and access

How do I start with someone?

Share an invitation. A connection starts when the other person accepts; searching for them does not connect you.

Why do I need to verify my email?

It confirms the address is yours. Open the link we sent, then return to the app. Check spam or request another link if needed.

Forgot your password?

Tap “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in screen. We send a link to your account email.

Cannot sign in with Google?

Check that you chose the Google account linked to your profile. If it fails, note the message and contact support; avoid creating a second account by accident.

Connections and safety

Does removing a connection delete our games?

No. Removing hides it from your list. Its history remains and may reappear if you search for the person by alias.

What does deleting nine-question rounds remove?

Only completed nine-question rounds, for both people. Active rounds, other games, chat and saved memories remain. It does not reset the free allowance.

What changes when I block someone?

Blocking stops new invitations and games between you. You can review or remove blocks in Settings.

What happens when I report a connection?

Interaction between you is paused while the case is reviewed. A report does not automatically close an account.

Does reporting a message also block the person?

No. It sends that item for review but does not block or pause the connection by itself. You can block separately.

Do we have to be a couple to play?

No. Your chosen relationship type helps show suitable activities. Romantic or Spicy options may have extra requirements.

Questions and guesses

What is One good question?

Choose one short question to start a conversation. You can also pick a nine-question round or play in person.

How do the 9 questions work?

Each of you chooses topics and confirms a mix. Then you answer nine questions separately and compare when they are revealed.

What do I guess in How well do you know me?

Guess a real detail the other person shared in their profile. There are three choices and one correct answer.

How does I bet you choose… work?

You each answer five dilemmas and guess the other person’s choices. Compare afterward; agreeing is not the goal.

Games

What is in Leisure?

Tic-tac-toe, Hangman, puzzles, and in-person play. If you both enabled Spicy, 21 and Dice may also appear.

How do I play Two truths and a lie?

Write three statements: two true and one made up. The other person tries to spot the lie.

How do I play tic-tac-toe?

Place one mark each turn. The first to get three in a row wins. You can return while the game is still available.

How do I play Hangman?

One person sets a word and a hint; the other guesses letters. You can swap roles afterward.

How do we play the shared puzzle?

Choose an illustration and piece count. Place one piece each turn; if rotation is on, orient it too.

How does Four-hand doodle work?

One person knows the prompt. Take turns drawing one continuous stroke and try to guess before time runs out.

How do we play 21 together?

Get close to 21 without going over. The loser gives their chosen forfeit to the winner. It needs Spicy and Premium or an extra.

How do Spicy Dice work?

Roll two dice: one says what to do, the other where. They need Spicy and Premium or an extra.

Can we play on one phone?

Yes. In-person mode has questions and turns on one device, without linking two accounts.

Things you create together

How do we make A story together?

Write four lines, alternating turns. Each line builds on what the other person wrote; there is no right answer.

What is Two views, one memory?

You each describe a shared memory, then compare how you remember it.

What is Our living list for?

Keep shared ideas—plans, films, songs, or places—so you can come back to them later.

What is From dream to first step?

Choose a hope and propose one concrete first step. It starts a conversation, not an automatic promise.

How does Secret mission work?

Choose a small gesture, do it without announcing it, and let your connection try to guess what it was.

What can I send in Details?

Choose a card or another available detail and add your words. Private photos have their own access and expiry rules.

Talking and listening

What is This is what I need for?

Say what happened, how you feel, and one small thing that would help. The other person may agree, suggest another option, or say no.

What happens in Really listening?

One person shares something specific; the other listens and sums up what they understood. Clarify, then swap turns.

What is Pause and return?

It helps you ask for a break and, if you both want, suggest when to talk again. Nobody is required to resume.

How do we make A decision together?

Pick a small decision, say what matters to each of you, and try an option that respects both boundaries.

What is A moment together?

Share a short moment without phones. Look at something together, sit quietly, or look at each other; either can stop.

Is Dr. Corazón therapy?

No. These are recreational listening and conversation exercises. They do not diagnose your relationship or replace professional help.

Today and shared plans

What is Today’s moment?

A short activity that changes. It may be a prediction or a doodle; open the card to see today’s version.

Why does Today suggest something?

Today shows waiting replies and games first. If nothing is pending, it suggests an activity; you can always choose another.

Where do my chat messages go?

They go to the connection you send them to. Chat is not a hidden game answer; it is delivered as a message.

What is shared when I import a calendar plan?

Only the event you choose: title, date, time, and location if present. Your whole calendar is not uploaded.

Your privacy

Can the other person see my answers early?

Not in hidden-answer games. Answers appear when the activity allows it. A chat message is delivered when sent.

Can I hide my profile photo?

Yes. Choose who can see it, or hide it, in your profile. A photo sent as a Detail has separate rules.

Are my mood and exercise feedback private?

Your personal check-in and optional exercise feedback are not shown as answers to your connection. Review each activity before sharing content.

Free access and purchases

When do free activities reset?

Each connection gets two free starts per day. They reset at midnight, Costa Rica time. Resuming an open activity does not start another.

What can I play without using those starts?

Tic-tac-toe, Hangman, Dr. Corazón, and the daily moment do not use those two starts.

What do Premium and an extra change?

Premium removes the daily limit for covered connections. An extra adds one start for a connection. Check the price in Google Play before paying.

Why can I not see Spicy?

Both people must be adults, have an eligible connection, and opt in. Some options also need Premium or an extra.

Paid but cannot see the benefit?

Check the Google Play account and tap “Restore purchases” in Premium. If it still fails, send us the order number, never card details.

Does deleting my account cancel Premium?

No. Cancel an active subscription in Google Play. Deleting your Conoche? account is a separate action.

Support

Where do I delete my account?

Open Settings > Account > Delete account. You will need to confirm your identity. Hiding a connection or signing out does not delete the account.

Request deletion without signing in
How do I contact support?

Email support@tikiziagames.com. The form saves a ticket in the app but does not email it automatically; the email button opens your email app.

Get support
What is hello@tikiziagames.com for?

It is for general or business inquiries. For account, payment, safety, or app issues, use support@tikiziagames.com.
