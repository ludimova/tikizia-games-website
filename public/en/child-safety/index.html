Conoche? · Tikizia Games

Child safety is not negotiable.

These standards apply to accounts, invitations, games, questions, chat, photos, drawings and all content shared in Conoche.

Updated: September 24, 2026

1. What is prohibited

Tikizia Games prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Creating, requesting, storing, promoting or distributing content that sexualizes, exploits or endangers a minor is prohibited, including digitally generated or altered content.

Grooming, sexual extortion, recruitment for exploitation, requesting intimate images from minors and lying about age to access adult features are prohibited. Spicy is only for adults who opt in; it is not an exception to these standards.

2. How to tell us

Use the in-app connection or content reporting options, or email support@tikiziagames.com with the subject “Child safety · Conoche?”. Include the alias or identifier, approximate date and where it happened. You can block the person separately.

Do not download or forward child sexual abuse material. Explain how to find the content in the app, without attaching it. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local authorities or emergency services.

3. How we respond

We review reports and the necessary context. When we identify child sexual abuse material, we remove it or restrict access, take action on the account and retain only what is necessary for investigation and legal obligations. We report cases to the appropriate authorities as required by applicable law.

We do not claim continuous monitoring or guarantee a fixed response time. An in-app report does not replace reporting to authorities or emergency assistance.

4. Child-safety contact

Tikizia Games · support@tikiziagames.com

This channel receives child-safety concerns and inquiries from authorities. For other app issues, visit support.
