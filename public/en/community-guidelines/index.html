Community guidelines

The point is to play with another person, not corner them. These rules apply to games, chat, photos, invitations, and all shared content.

Updated: September 24, 2026

1. No pressure

No means no, including during a game. Nobody has to answer a question, share a photo, accept a dare, enable Spicy, or keep playing to please someone else. Do not use the app to harass, intimidate, or push past a boundary.

2. Protect others

Threats, discrimination, blackmail, impersonation, exposing another person’s information, and illegal content are not allowed. Do not distribute someone else’s messages, drawings, or photos without permission. A recipient may capture what you send; consider that before sharing.

3. Spicy is for adults who opt in

Do not use 18+ features with minors or misstate your age. Both adults must opt in to Spicy, and either may turn it off. Do not send unsolicited sexual content or use purchases or rewards as pressure.

4. Hide, block, and report

Hiding a connection removes it from your list only. Blocking stops new invitations and interaction between you. Reporting a connection pauses it during review; reporting one message or detail does not automatically pause or block it. These controls can be used separately.

5. How a case is reviewed

We may review a report and the limited context attached by the feature, request more information, and take proportionate action. A report does not automatically close an account. We do not promise to resolve every case within a fixed time. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

6. Action and follow-up

Depending on the case, we may warn, restrict features, suspend, or close an account. A subscription does not exempt anyone. Bad-faith reporting to punish someone is not allowed. If you think we made a mistake, email support@tikiziagames.com with context, without forwarding unnecessary intimate material.

Child safety

Conoche? and Tikizia Games prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sexual extortion. Producing, requesting, storing or sharing this content is prohibited. These standards also apply to digitally generated or altered content.
