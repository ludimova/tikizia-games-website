ABOUT TIKIZIA

Costa Rican soul. Games with sabor.

Tikizia Games is a growing mobile game studio born in Costa Rica.

We create games centered around real human moments: love, friendships, parties, questions, memories and laughs.

We want our games to feel easy to play, beautiful to use and memorable to share.

Our goal is simple: make games people actually want to open, replay and talk about.

FAQ · TIKIZIA GAMES

Questions? Probably.

What is Tikizia Games?

An independent Costa Rican game studio creating playful social experiences for couples, friends and groups. Conoche and Vinazo are part of our family of games.

Where is Tikizia Games based?

Tikizia Games is based in Costa Rica.

What games are you working on?

Conoche, for questions, games and shared moments; Vinazo, around stories, memories and friends; and more ideas on the way.

Where can I find help with Conoche?

Visit Conoche for its introduction, FAQs, support, policies and tester access.

How do I contact support?

Email support@tikiziagames.com with the game name and what happened. Do not send passwords or sign-in links.

Are more games coming?

Absolutely. Conoche and Vinazo are just the beginning.
