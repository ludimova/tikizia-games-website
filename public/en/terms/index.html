1. Account and age

Connected features require your own account and accurate information. Email sign-up requires verification. Do not use someone else’s account. Features marked 18+ are for adults only; do not enter a false age to unlock them.

2. Voluntary connections

A connection starts when the other person accepts an invitation. It does not require either person to play, answer, chat, or stay in a relationship. Either person can stop, hide the connection from their list, or block the other. Hiding does not delete its history.

3. Games and shared content

The app includes questions, turn-based games, exercises, missions, details, chat, and plans. Each activity explains when content is sent or revealed. You are responsible for what you write, draw, or send. You retain your rights in that content and allow Conoche? to store and display it as needed to provide the feature and review reports.

4. Respect and adult content

Respect the other person’s boundaries. Harassment, threats, impersonation, sexual pressure, and illegal content are not allowed. Spicy requires two adults in an eligible connection who both opt in. Either can turn it off. A purchase does not replace those conditions or consent.

5. Suggestions and exercises

Conoche? recommendations and exercises are recreational. They do not evaluate a relationship or replace therapy, medical care, professional advice, or emergency help. You can skip an activity or choose another.

6. Free access, Premium, and extras

The app shows which activities are free, which use a daily start, and what requires Premium or an extra. Google Play shows the scope, price, and terms of a purchase before confirmation. Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play; deleting a Conoche? account does not automatically cancel it.

7. Reports and action

You can block someone or report a connection or specific content. Reporting a connection pauses interaction between you; reporting one item does not automatically pause the connection. Tikizia Games may review the context and issue warnings, restrictions, suspension, or closure as appropriate. A report alone does not prove wrongdoing; bad-faith reports are also prohibited.

8. Account, history, and deletion

You can manage your profile, visibility, blocks, and available deletion options in the app. Removing a connection from your list only hides it. Account deletion is a separate, permanent action requiring identity confirmation and may affect data shared with others. The Privacy Policy explains deletion and limited retention.

9. Changes and availability

We may fix errors, change or remove features, and adjust limits to maintain the service and its safety. For material changes affecting purchases, data handling, or rights, we will update the relevant information and request renewed acceptance when appropriate. We do not promise uninterrupted availability.

10. Contact

For account, safety, content, or payment issues, email support@tikiziagames.com. For general or business inquiries, use hello@tikiziagames.com.

Child safety

Conoche? and Tikizia Games prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sexual extortion. Producing, requesting, storing or sharing this content is prohibited. These standards also apply to digitally generated or altered content.
