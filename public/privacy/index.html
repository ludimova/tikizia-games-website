TIKIZIA GAMES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tikizia Games collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our websites, mobile games and related services.

Tikizia Games is a Costa Rican game brand operated by Luis Diego Mora.

This policy applies to Tikizia Games products, including Conoche, and to future Tikizia Games products unless a product provides a separate privacy policy.

1. Information We May Collect

The information we collect depends on the Tikizia Games product and the features you use.

Account information

When you create or use an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Display name or username
  • Profile avatar or profile photo
  • Account identifiers
  • Date of birth and age-status declaration used to apply age-restricted features
  • Authentication information provided by supported sign-in providers

Game and connection information

Social games such as Conoche may store information needed to provide multiplayer or connected experiences, including:

  • Connections between players
  • Invite codes
  • Game rounds and progress
  • Answers and responses submitted during gameplay
  • Selected game categories or preferences
  • Game results and related activity

Purchases

If you purchase digital products, subscriptions or premium features, transactions may be processed by platforms such as Google Play.

Tikizia Games may receive transaction identifiers, purchase status and information necessary to provide purchased features. We do not receive your complete credit card information from Google Play.

Technical and diagnostic information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate, secure and improve our products, such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • App version
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Security and authentication events
  • Push-notification registration tokens and device identifiers needed to deliver app notifications

Safety and moderation information

When safety tools are used, we may store limited information necessary to investigate and respond to abuse, including:

  • Blocks between users
  • Reports and the reason selected by the reporter
  • Optional report details and references to the relevant connection, round, question or response
  • Safety holds or moderation status associated with a report

Safety records may be retained for a limited period after an account is deleted when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, prevent fraud, protect users, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

Support communications

If you contact us, we may receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide game functionality
  • Connect players who choose to connect with each other
  • Save game progress and responses
  • Provide purchases and premium features
  • Protect accounts and prevent abuse or fraud
  • Provide customer support
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve our games and services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Service Providers

Tikizia Games may use third-party service providers to operate its games and services.

These may include services provided by Google and Firebase for functions such as authentication, cloud data storage, application infrastructure and diagnostics, as well as Google Play for distribution and purchases.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual protections.

For Conoche? account verification and password reset emails, Resend processes the recipient address and message content. Resend retains its copy and delivery logs for 30 days; it does not control or delete messages in a recipient's inbox.

4. Sharing of Information

Tikizia Games does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, including with service providers, when required by law, to protect users or our services, or when you intentionally share information with another player through a game feature.

5. Connected Players

Some Tikizia Games products are designed around interaction between players.

Certain information, responses or gameplay results may therefore be visible to another player when that visibility is part of the game experience.

We aim to make these interactions clear within the relevant product.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account functionality, comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, protect our services and enforce our terms.

When an account is deleted, account-associated personal data will be deleted or anonymized unless limited information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or financial-record purposes.

Limited copies may remain temporarily in backups as part of normal backup and disaster-recovery processes.

For account-email security and support, Conoche? keeps a limited record of accepted sends for approximately one year: recipient address, message type, date and provider reference. This record does not include password-reset or verification links, one-time codes, or message content.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Tikizia Games account and associated account data.

Account deletion may be initiated through supported Tikizia Games apps or through our external account deletion page:

tikiziagames.com/delete-account/

You may also contact:

support@tikiziagames.com

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Tikizia Games products are not directed to children under 13.

Individual games may have additional age requirements depending on their content and features.

10. International Users

Tikizia Games is operated from Costa Rica. Depending on the services used to provide our products, information may be processed or stored in other countries.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our games, services or legal obligations change.

When we update it, we will revise the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
