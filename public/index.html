ABOUT TIKIZIA

Costa Rican soul. Games with sabor.

Tikizia Games is a growing mobile game studio born in Costa Rica.

We create games centered around real human moments: love, friendships, parties, questions, memories and laughs.

We want our games to feel easy to play, beautiful to use and memorable to share.

Our goal is simple: make games people actually want to open, replay and talk about.

FAQ

Questions? Probably.

What is Tikizia Games?

Tikizia Games is an independent Costa Rican game studio focused on playful social experiences for couples, friends and groups.

Where is Tikizia Games based?

Tikizia Games is based in Costa Rica.

What games are you working on?

Our first projects include Conoche, a couples game, and Vinazo, a social game built around stories, memories and friends.

How do I contact support?

You can contact us at support@tikiziagames.com.

How can I delete my account?

You can request account deletion by contacting support@tikiziagames.com. Account deletion options may also be available directly inside supported Tikizia games.

Are more games coming?

Absolutely. Conoche and Vinazo are just the beginning.
